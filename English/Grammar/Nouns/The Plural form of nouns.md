The Plural of Nouns:

## Most nouns from the plural by adding -s or -es:

Examples:
```text
boat => boats
hat => hats
house => houses
river => rivers
```

## Words that end in -ch, x, s or s-like sounds, however, will require an -es for the plural:

Examples:
```text
witch => witches
box => boxes
gas => gases
bus => buses
kiss => kisses
Jones => Joneses
```

Note: There's one exception to this rule. If the -ch ending is pronounced with 'k' sound, you add -s rather than -es:

```text
stomach => stomachs
epoh => epohs
```

## A noun ending in -y preceded by a consonant makes the plural with -ies:

```text
a cry => cries
a fly => flies
a nappy => nappies
a poppy => poppies
a city => cities
a lady => ladies
a baby => babies
```

## Nouns ending in -f or -fe:

With nouns that end in a consonant or a single vowel plus -f or -fe, to -ves:

**... consonant + (-f or -fe) => -ves**
**... single vowel + (-f or -fe) => -ves**

```text
knife => knives
half => halves
scraf => scarves
```

Nouns with end in two vowels plus -f usually from plurals in the normal way, with just an -s

**... x2 vowels + -f => -s**

```text
shief => shiefs
spoof => spoofs
```

## Some nouns have the same form in the singular and the plural:

Examples:

```text
sheep => sheep
fish => fish
species => species
aircraft => aircraft
```

## Some nouns have a plural form but take a singular verb:

**news** => The **news is** at 6.30 p.m.
**athletics** => **Athletics is** good for young people
**linguistics** => **Linguistics is** the study of language
**darts** => **Darts is** a popular game in England
**billiards** => **Billiards is ** played all over the world

## Some nouns have a plural form and take a plural verb:

**trousers** => My **trousers are** to tight
**jeans** => Her **jeans are** black
**glasses** => Those **glasses are** his

others include:
```text
savings, thanks, steps, stair, customs, congratulations, tropics, wages, spectacles, outskirts, goods, wits.
```

## Pronounced /s/ (like a sharp 's'):
Use when the word ends in a **voiceless** sound (your vocal cords don't vibrate) like /p/, /t/, /k/, /f/, /o/. (th sound)
Examples `stops, hats, books, roofs`

## Pronounced /z/ (like buzzing 'z'):
Use when the word ends in a **voiced** sound (your vocal cords vibrate) like /b/, /d/, /g/, /v/, /m/, /n/, or any vowel sound
Examples: `dogs, loves, runs, plays, knives`

## Pronounced /iz/ (like "iz"):
Use when the word ends in a **hissing** or **buzzing** sound:
/s/, /z/, /S/(sh), /tS/(ch), /dz/(j), /ks/(x).
This adds extra syllable
Examples: `misses, couses, watches, changes, boxes, faxes`


# Test yourself

## Part 1. Regular Plurals: -s or -es (1-5)

**1.** What is the plural of **box**?  
a) boxs  
b) box  
c) boxes

> [!NOTE]- Question 1
> **Correct answer:** c) boxes  
> *Words ending in **-x** require **-es** for the plural: box → boxes.*

**2.** Choose the correct plural:  
_"Three ___ are swimming in the river."_  
a) fishs  
b) fishes  
c) fish

> [!NOTE]- Question 2
> **Correct answer:** c) fish  
> *The noun **fish** has the same form in singular and plural: one fish, three fish. (Though **fishes** exists for multiple species, the basic plural is **fish**.)*

**3.** Which word takes **-es** in the plural?  
a) hat  
b) kiss  
c) boat

> [!NOTE]- Question 3
> **Correct answer:** b) kiss  
> *Words ending in **-ss** take **-es**: kiss → kisses.*

**4.** What is the plural of **stomach**?  
a) stomaches  
b) stomachs  
c) stomacks

> [!NOTE]- Question 4
> **Correct answer:** b) stomachs  
> *If the **-ch** ending is pronounced with a /k/ sound, we add only **-s**: stomach → stomachs.*

**5.** Fill in the blank:  
_"The ___ (Jones) are coming to dinner."_  
a) Jones  
b) Joneses  
c) Jone's

> [!NOTE]- Question 5
> **Correct answer:** b) Joneses  
> *Proper nouns ending in **-s** (like surnames) take **-es**: Jones → Joneses.*
## Part 2. Plurals with -y and -f/-fe (6-9)

**6.** What is the plural of **city**?  
a) citys  
b) cities  
c) cityes

> [!NOTE]- Question 6
> **Correct answer:** b) cities  
> *A noun ending in **-y** preceded by a consonant changes **-y** to **-ies**: city → cities.*

**7.** Choose the correct plural:  
_"Put the ___ on the table."_ (knife)  
a) knifes  
b) knifes  
c) knives

> [!NOTE]- Question 7
> **Correct answer:** c) knives  
> *Nouns ending in **-fe** change to **-ves**: knife → knives.*

a) half → halves  
b) cliff → clives  
c) life → lives

> [!NOTE]- Question 8
> **Correct answer:** b) cliff → cliffs
> *Cliff ends in two consonants + **-f**, so it adds regular **-s**: cliff → cliffs. **Clives** is incorrect.*

**9.** Fill in the blank:  
_"He told us two funny ___."_ (story)  
a) storys  
b) stories  
c) storyes

> [!NOTE]- Question 9
> **Correct answer:** b) stories  
> *Story ends in consonant + **-y**, so plural is **stories**.*
